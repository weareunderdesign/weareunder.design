## under
2017

i'm eden. i run a design studio with my wife inbal. it's called under. one of us came from code, the other from brand. we run it from the third floor of our house in atlit. three girls. monday to thursday.

everything i do is design. i write code, it's design. i talk with someone, it's design. it's all bad design, good design, or remarkable design. no design is also design.

there's a gap. between thinking about a thing, being the thing, and doing the thing. most of design lives in that gap. revisions. more revisions. can we make it pop. months of it. and it leaves you with something fine. fine is not memorable.

closing that gap is our whole thing.

one day inbal said, what if we just give it ten days. and i said no, this is emotional, it needs time. and as i was saying it i was like, oh my god, i'm lying to myself. everyone are lying. time was never what we were protecting. it was the excuse. the gap was the excuse.

so we cut it. with no time for revisions there's no time to be fine. you can be cautious or you can be creative. whatever you make is ninety-nine percent like everything else anyway. the one percent weird is the whole difference. different is better than better.

and something happens when the time is short. at the piano you stop thinking about the piano. you and it become one thing. thinking, being, doing. one motion. that's what we go after. not the output.

but you can't close the gap with a deadline alone. me, you, or santana. same guitar. the tool isn't the difference. yet we create tools and then they shape us. so we make our own. things that make things. the ten days became one. we called it brandsprint. a hundred brands went through it. a byproduct. the urge is upstream.

what closes the gap is knowledge, not property. so we work in the open. the process, the drafts, the failures. work you never show turns into regret.

you are not what you think you are. you are not what you say you are. you are what you do. the being becomes the doing.

atlit. three girls. the work. the same day. there is no boundary. there is only creation.

one plus one equals three. every day.
