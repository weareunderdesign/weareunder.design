## under
2017

i'm eden. i run a design studio with my wife inbal. it's called under. one of us came from code, the other from brand. we run it from the third floor of our house in atlit. three girls. monday to thursday.

everything i do is design. i write code, it's design. i talk with someone, it's design. it's all bad design, good design, or remarkable design. no design is also design.

so we looked at how design gets made. revisions. more revisions. can we make it pop. months of it. and it leaves you with something fine. fine is not memorable.

the work is fine. the way it gets made is broken.

one day inbal said, what if we do a sprint for branding. and i said no, branding is emotional, it needs time. and as i was saying it i was like, oh my god, i'm lying to myself. everyone are lying.

since then, a hundred brands. ten days each.

ten days sounds cautious. it's the opposite. you can be cautious or you can be creative. with no time for revisions there's no time to be fine. your identity is going to be ninety-nine percent like everything else anyway. the one percent weird is the whole difference. different is better than better. half great beats full mediocre.

and something happens in ten days that months never gave us. at the piano you stop thinking about the piano. you and it become one thing. no gap between idea and execution. that gap is what we go after. not the output.

me, you, or santana. the guitar is the same guitar. the tool isn't the difference. but we create tools and then they shape us. so we make things that make things. our own first.

what closes the gap is knowledge, not property. so we work in the open. the process, the drafts, the failures. work you never show turns into regret.

because a brand is not a logo. it's your being. and the sprint is where the being becomes the doing.

atlit. three girls. the work. the same day. there is no boundary. there is only creation.

one plus one equals three. every day.
