## the talk, the important things
brandsprint — branding is broken. eden's words from the stage, cleaned. the full 10k-word transcript lives in under/brandsprint history at bd5428a.

### who
i run a design studio with my wife inbal. it's called under. third floor of our house in atlit. three girls. monday to thursday. brand products for technology companies.

lazy perfectionist with adhd. idealist fighting a pragmatist.

### design
everything i do is design. i write code, it's design. i talk with someone, it's design. it's all bad design, good design, or remarkable design. no design is also design.

the world is chaotic. we make some order in it.

creativity is the act of discovery. every block of stone has a statue inside it.

simplicity and complexity. a guitar: i play it, santana plays it, same guitar. low floor, no ceiling. the key to balancing them is openness.

we create tools and then they shape us. everything around us except nature was designed by someone. i make things that make things. meta creativity: not just create, empower others to create.

beautiful things are efficiency that reached a height we did not see before.

heroes: leonardo, the og full stack designer. teenage engineering. hey studio. neri oxman. sagmeister & walsh — always 1% weird. they respect controversy.

### the lie
i climbed my own ladder. code, drawing, writing, marketing, yoga, music. it only got real when i started making tools and my own startup.

two girls, a third on the way, no money in the bank, covid. inbal said: what if we do the sprint for branding. i said no, branding is emotional, it needs time. as i was saying it: oh my god, i'm lying to myself. everyone are lying.

the business lesson: focus on a niche and serve them really really well. make a small number of people very very happy.

since then, five years, a hundred brands. ten days each.

### what a brand is
not a logo. not a product. your being. the soul of your idea, fragmented into things people can touch, see, feel.

nike doesn't sell shoes, they hijack victory. coca-cola doesn't sell sugar water, they sell time machines. weak brands fade. strong brands colonize your mind.

a brand is not the dream. it's the daily ground. founders preach it, designers obsess over pixels, developers bleed in the code. they all become priests of the brand. the logo is just the crown. the real worship happens in a thousand tiny acts, every day.

the being becomes the doing.

### what's broken
a slow train. bloated, outdated, stuck in an endless loop of meetings, revisions, indecision. startups move at lightning speed and leave it in the dust.

no momentum. projects drag for months. by the time you decide, the excitement is gone.
too many choices, no conviction. a thousand logos on the wall. designed by a group of managers. safe, bland, forgettable.
expensive, inefficient. a shiny deck that collects dust. strategy without execution is useless. a lot of doing without being.
bureaucracy kills bold ideas. revisions. more revisions. can we make it pop. it leaves you with something fine. fine is not memorable.

### the fix
momentum is everything. ten days. keeps excitement alive and decisions sharp. progress, not paralysis.

you can be cautious or you can be creative. i believe in people's ability to create chaos on purpose and then make order from it. it needs boundaries. it needs tough love. it needs a hyper talented team.

one clear direction. no endless options. one bold identity with total conviction. strong brands don't second guess themselves.

a product, not a process. simple and flexible. you can break it a bit, but some rules are written in blood. agencies sell branding as a never-ending service. we treat it as a premium product.

the big reveal. we agree on the story, then we disappear. we come back as the experts. bigger than the sum of its parts.

the slow bloated agency model is dead. branding isn't about looking good. it's about making an impact and claiming your place in people's minds.

### the ten days
strategy. three hours. timers, sticky notes, a mess of questions. golden circle: start with why. the personality exercise is the best one — rebel or conventional, elite or mass, it starts a debate. tone of voice becomes look and feel.

story. no visuals yet. there is no truth, but you need a story. a good brand is a story reiterated all the time. weak story, weak brand, bad business. a strong name makes the story. keep the unimportant details, they tell you who the founder is.

research. the secret sauce is the 1% weird. your identity is going to be 99% like everything else. the 1% surprise is the difference between good and remarkable. different is better than better.

lucian freud: i want each picture to be the only picture i'm working on, the only picture i've ever worked on, the only picture anyone has ever done.

references: start with a lot, remove until two or three remain. by then you're not copying anymore. it's entirely yours. they're an anchor so you remember where you came from.

design. the dance. style guide in the center, posters on one side, website on the other. decide, apply, bring it back. one board. every night the master sprinter archives everything that isn't continuing. next day, a clean canvas.

a to z. start big, become laser focused. throw everything, no judgment, quantity over quality. then say what's not continuing. keep the best, let go of the rest. abc, still different, refined once more. then the hardest point: put away good things, keep the one. refine the corner radius, the tone. a winner.

but fuck the method if you have a hunch. we trust our gut. it's yoga, it stretches the mind. it's just a method.

colors. i'm ruthless. black and white first. it has to work in grayscale. only then i've earned the right to pick a color. sometimes the color is obvious from day one — vision camp: not the camp, the vision, so blue.

weekend. keep running. don't stop.

reveal. paul rand and steve jobs and next. one option, you pay for it, use it if you like. the question every day and on the way to their office on day ten: one plus one equals three. we get the design to its highest fidelity. only then can the client ruin it. no hard feelings after, because they already see the three in one plus one.

purify. last changes. hand off. sensitive — they don't know what to do with what you just made. give it to them in the way they can use it. founders preach, developers build, marketers mix and match.

handshake. fade out. then brand scale: not one more page, but a big stone we can move together.

### in every gray b2b product
there is something underneath that is very colorful, very weird, that you can double down on. it can only happen in a short amount of time.

### rhythm
don't sprint and sprint and sprint. sprint, rest, repeat. a marathon you don't feel. a sprint you feel.

we make more tools as a byproduct. they become their own things. human computer interaction, creativity interaction — why we all started using computers in the first place.

i swear i'm going to help you somehow.
